Seeking knowledge is the duty of every believer, and as Muslim Parents, it's our Core responsibility to teach ourselves and our children the teachings of the Qur'an and Islam, as this will serve as a guide for the rulings of Life.

Due to the hectic schedule in this modern world, many of us can't easily deal with these basic obligations. To address this, Riyadul Quran Academy has brought this opportunity to every individual in the comfort of their own home.

We invite you to join our growing community of learners from Nigeria, USA, Canada, Britain and beyond, and start your journey of spiritual growth and knowledge today.
